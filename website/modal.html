Modal

Kioto styles the overlay and dialog surface; your application owns focus management, dismissal, and state.

Attribute-driven state

Set data-state="open" on the overlay. Move focus inside on open, trap it, restore it on close, and close on Escape.

Archive project?

The project will become read-only and move out of active views.
